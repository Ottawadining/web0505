Which Ottawa restaurants are open for lunch?

Downtown Ottawa can feel like a food desert at noon if you don't know where to look. It isn't — you just need the right block.

  • Cocotte Bistro

    Metcalfe Street · Inside the Metcalfe Hotel

    The elevated option on this list — modern French classics like duck confit, escargot and moules frites, in a room spacious enough for a full executive lunch or a quiet one-on-one.

  • Toro Taqueria

    Bank Street

    Tacos, burritos and bowls with everything made in-house — meats, sauces, the works. A reliable, fast option when the craving is specifically Mexican.

  • Bread and Sons

    Bank Street · Bakery and sandwich counter

    Known for pastries, but the lunch menu of focaccia sandwiches and pizza by the slice is just as strong — heavy on vegetarian toppings like roasted peppers, olives and potatoes.

  • Paradise Poké

    Bank Street · Order ahead recommended

    Build-your-own poke bowls with sushi-grade fish, rice or zucchini noodles, and a long list of toppings. Reliably busy at noon — ordering online ahead of time saves the wait.

  • Seoul Dog

    O'Connor Street

    Korean-style corn dogs well beyond the childhood version — half-cheese, half-meat fillings, breaded and rolled in everything from sugar to crushed ramen. A fun, fast detour from the usual lunch rotation.

  • Mulligans Golf Bar

    Queen Street

    Well-executed pub comfort food — burgers, nachos, fish and chips, chicken and waffles — for the days a proper sit-down lunch is the whole point.

Most of these cluster in the same few downtown blocks between Wellington and Somerset, so a short walk can cover several options if nothing sounds right on the first pass.
