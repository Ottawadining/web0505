Which Ottawa restaurants are good for a special occasion?

A special occasion needs a room that feels like one — these have the atmosphere to match whatever you're celebrating.

  • Riviera

    Sparks Street

    A former bank turned art-deco dining room, with an open kitchen, handmade pasta and a well-regarded bar program. Few rooms in the city announce "occasion" as clearly the moment you walk in.

  • Gitanes

    Centretown, Elgin Street · Chef's Table $145, tasting menu from $105

    French cuisine built on Canadian ingredients, a raw bar, and one of the city's more extensive wine cellars — consistently named among Ottawa's most romantic dining rooms, with a kitchen-side Chef's Table for anyone who wants the full production.

  • Giovanni's Ristorante

    Little Italy

    Refined, seasonal Italian cooking in a room built for date nights — crystal glassware, polished service and a wine list deep enough to match the occasion.

  • Atelier

    Little Italy · Seven courses, $250 per person, Wed–Sat

    A restaurant that serves nothing but its own tasting menu — a seven-course format built around molecular-gastronomy technique, roughly three hours long. About as dedicated an occasion as a meal in this city gets.

  • Le St. Laurent

    St. Laurent Boulevard

    The occasion here is largely the view — a dining room built around it, smart-casual and dinner-only, for a night that doesn't need a scene to feel special.

Rooms like these fill up around anniversaries, holidays and weekends — book further out than you think you need to.
