Which Ottawa restaurants have private dining rooms?

A handful of Ottawa restaurants keep a dedicated room set aside for exactly this — sized anywhere from an intimate table for eight to a full 250-guest buyout.

  • Beckta Dining & Wine

    Downtown, Elgin Street · Five named rooms, 8 to 36 guests

    The most options on this list: the intimate Juliette and Cigar rooms, the Gloucester Room (up to 12), the Elgin Room (up to 16), and the Grant Room, which seats up to 28 on its own or 36 across three combined tables. Menus are customized per event.

  • North & Navy

    Centretown · Private room, vintage-inspired decor

    A converted brick house turned Northern Italian bacaro, with a private room regularly booked for rehearsal dinners and small celebrations — patterned rug, leather seating, and the same handmade pasta menu as the main room.

  • Métropolitain Brasserie

    ByWard Market, Sussex Drive · Private room seats up to 140

    The largest dedicated private room in this list, inside a Parisian-style brasserie with the city's largest oyster bar. Built to handle everything from a business dinner to a full-scale reception.

  • Parlour

    Hintonburg, Wellington West · Event space, up to 250 guests

    Less a private room than a private venue — a mid-century-modern space that hosts curated events of any size, from small private dinners to full buyouts, alongside its regular chef-driven, farm-to-table menu.

  • The Shore Club

    ByWard Market · The Rideau Room, up to 18 guests

    An intimate, art-lined private room inside this seafood-and-steak dining room, with its own stereo and AV setup — built as much for a business dinner as a celebration.

  • Stofa

    Hintonburg

    A private room for both small and larger groups, available for professional meetings as well as celebrations, alongside Stofa's regular table d'hôte menu.

Room availability and minimums shift with the season and the size of your group — it's worth calling ahead rather than assuming a room is free.
