Which Ottawa restaurants are open on Sundays?

Sunday closes more of Ottawa's dining rooms than you'd expect — including several well-known ones — but a solid list stays open for brunch, dinner, or both.

  • Wilf & Ada's

    Centretown · Saturday & Sunday, 8 a.m.–2 p.m.

    The scratch diner most Ottawa brunch lists put first — house-smoked bacon, fresh-squeezed juice, made-from-scratch hollandaise. Walk-in only, and the Sunday line often starts before opening.

  • Wilfrid's, Fairmont Château Laurier

    Downtown · Weekend brunch, Sat & Sun 11:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.

    A grander option for the same day: a family-style brunch buffet overlooking Parliament and the canal locks, with breakfast, lunch and dinner also served daily.

  • Métropolitain Brasserie

    ByWard Market · Sunday, 10 a.m.–11 p.m.

    Open all day on Sundays, from a 10 a.m. brunch through to a full dinner service — Ottawa's largest raw bar, classic brasserie plates, and a wine list built for lingering.

  • Chesterfield's Gastro Diner

    Wellington West · Open daily except Tuesdays

    A self-described "not so greasy spoon" serving brunch every day it's open, with a Jamaican-inflected eggs benny and chicken and waffles that regulars order on repeat.

  • Fauna

    Centretown · Sunday dinner, 5:30–10 p.m.

    A Bank Street fixture that keeps its Sunday hours nearly identical to the rest of the week — seasonal Canadian plates, a lively room, and a kitchen in no hurry to wind down for the weekend.

  • Le St. Laurent

    St. Laurent Boulevard · Dinner Wednesday through Sunday

    The draw here is the view as much as the plate — a dining room built around it, and a calmer, smart-casual pick for a Sunday evening that doesn't need a scene to feel special.

  • Chez Lucien

    ByWard Market · Open seven days a week

    A cozy, low-key option for Sunday dinner when the evening calls for something simpler — burgers, mains and starters in a Market corner that never quite closes for the week.

Sunday hours shift more than any other day of the week, especially around holidays — it's worth a quick call before you go.
