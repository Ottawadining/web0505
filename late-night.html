Where can I eat in Ottawa after 10 p.m.?

Ottawa's kitchens close earlier than most cities its size, but a real handful stay open well past ten — from a ByWard Market pub to a Chinatown pho counter that keeps simmering toward sunrise.

  • Elgin Street Diner

    Centretown · Open 24 hours, every day

    A fixture on Elgin Street long before Ottawa's late-night scene took shape, still serving classic diner plates — poutine, all-day breakfast, burgers — around the clock, every day of the year.

  • Kettleman's Bagel

    Bank Street · Open 24 hours

    Not a sit-down meal, but a genuine late-night institution: a wood-fired bakery pulling Montreal-style bagels fresh at any hour, seven days a week.

  • Chez Lucien

    ByWard Market · Open until 2 a.m. nightly, kitchen serving into the early hours

    A cozy corner of the Market for burgers, mains and starters well after most restaurants have closed — better suited to a low-key late dinner than a bar crawl.

  • The Brig Pub

    ByWard Market · Kitchen and bar until 2 a.m., nightly

    A proper pub menu rather than just bar snacks, paired with cocktails and the occasional DJ — for anyone who wants an actual meal alongside a later night out.

  • Thr33's Co. Snack Bar

    The Glebe · Open until 2 a.m. daily

    Globally-inspired small plates and inventive cocktails in an intimate, dimly-lit room on Bank Street. Walk-ins only — no reservations.

  • Shawarma Palace

    Rideau Street · Open until 1 a.m. weeknights, 2 a.m. Friday and Saturday

    An Ottawa benchmark since 1997, and the anchor of the city's late-night shawarma strip near Rideau Street.

  • 3 Brothers Shawarma & Poutine

    Rideau Street · Open until 3 a.m. weeknights, 4 a.m. Friday and Saturday

    A few doors down from Shawarma Palace, and one of the latest kitchens in the city — best known for a shawarma-topped poutine built for the walk home.

  • New Pho Bo Ga La

    Chinatown · Open until roughly 1 a.m. weeknights, later on weekends

    A Somerset Street pho counter that keeps simmering long after Chinatown's other kitchens have closed. Weekend hours can run later still — worth calling ahead to confirm.

  • Somewhere Dine Bar

    ByWard Market · Open until 2 a.m., nightly

    Seafood and small plates in the middle of the Market's nightlife strip, for a later dinner that still feels like a proper sit-down meal.

Hours shift with the seasons and with ownership, so it's worth a quick call before you head out for anything past midnight.
