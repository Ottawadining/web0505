What are Ottawa's most exciting restaurants right now?

Not a ranking — just the kitchens making Ottawa's dining scene feel like it's actually moving right now.

  • Ruby's

    ByWard Market, Clarendon Courtyard · Opened June 2026

    The second restaurant from the team behind Old Ottawa South's long-running Belmont, tucked into a quiet cobblestone courtyard just off York Street. A roughly 70-bottle wine list with a natural streak, warm service, and a Sunday brunch already drawing a following after only a few months open.

  • Le Mien Craft Noodle

    ByWard Market, William Street

    Hand-pulled noodles made to order right at the front window, with several styles to choose from — the traditional beef noodle soup is the standard order, and portions run generous.

  • Gyubee Japanese Grill

    Merivale Road · All-you-can-eat yakiniku

    Tabletop grills and a long list of meat, seafood and vegetables to cook yourself, buffet-style — less a quiet dinner than a genuinely fun night out with a group.

  • Antheia

    Somerset West

    Chef Briana Kim's fermentation-focused follow-up to her acclaimed Alice — a chef's-counter restaurant running on its own fermentation calendar, built from housemade misos, garums and shoyus rather than a seasonal menu.

This one moves faster than most pages on this site — expect it to look different in six months, on purpose.
